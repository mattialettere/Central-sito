Features

Everything Central does, feature by feature

Central brings together three things that usually live in separate apps: a private, end-to-end encrypted chat, personal safety tools and a social space shared only with your contacts. Here is the full list, with what is free and what needs a plan.

Download for iPhone Download for Android

Updated on 24 November 2026

In short: Central is a free app for iPhone and Android that combines end-to-end encrypted chat, voice calls, groups and communities with personal safety tools: SOS, an "I'm OK" check-in, Safe Trip and safe zones. It has no ads. The Plus and Premium plans raise the limits, while the essential safety features stay free.

Talking: chats, groups and calls

Messaging works the way you expect from a modern chat app, with one important difference: content is encrypted on the sender's phone and only opens on the recipient's phone. How the encryption works is explained on the encrypted chat page.

One-to-one and group chats

  • Messages, photos, videos, voice notes and files, end-to-end encrypted.
  • Group chats with admins.
  • Disappearing messages, polls and silent blocking of contacts.
  • Scheduled messages and broadcasts (Plus and Premium plans).

Shared whiteboards and maps

Every conversation can have one or more whiteboards to write, draw and pin photos together in real time, and export them as an image. Shared maps collect places, addresses and meet-ups with an arrival area, so everyone can see who has already arrived.

Voice calls

Audio calls between Central users, including between iPhone and Android. Audio travels encrypted between the two phones with DTLS-SRTP, the WebRTC standard. Central does not offer video calls.

Personal safety

The safety features are designed for people who live alone, come home late, work on the move or want to know a relative has arrived. None of them is active without the consent of the person involved, who can disconnect at any time.

SOS

Sends your emergency contacts your location, your battery level and, when started from the app, a 30-second video. On iPhone it also starts with Siri ("Hey Siri, Central SOS") and from Control Center; on Android from the widget and the Quick Settings tile. Details are on the SOS and emergencies page.

"I'm OK" check-in

You agree on a time with the people who follow you. One tap confirms you are fine; if the confirmation does not arrive by the deadline, they get an alert. The check runs on the server, so the alert goes out even if your phone is off or flat.

Safe Trip and walk mode

Pick a destination and up to 5 people who follow you along the way. If you do not arrive by the expected time, they get an alarm. Walk mode works without a destination. Trip locations are encrypted and deleted from the server 24 hours after the trip ends.

Safe zones (geofences)

An agreed area such as home, school or work. Your followers get an alert when you leave and when you come back. A zone can only be created or changed with both people's agreement.

Points of interest and event log

Points of interest show the nearest emergency facilities without alerting anyone. The event log collects confirmations, missed check-ins, arrivals, SOS alerts and zone exits.

Web dashboard for followers

The web dashboard shows alerts, check-ins, ongoing trips and safe zones on a computer, together with the encrypted chat. The live map in the dashboard requires Plus or Premium. It is the same tool businesses use for lone workers.

Social, only with people you know

  • Gallery: share photos only with your contacts, with likes and comments, and exclude specific people from a photo.
  • Communities: spaces around an interest with posts, events and discussions, moderation and reporting.
  • Central City: an isometric town where every friend has a house to furnish, with coins earned by using the app. You can try it in the browser without signing up.

Platforms and languages

  • iPhone: App Store, in Italian and English.
  • Android: Google Play, in Italian, English, Spanish, German, French and Albanian.
  • Computer: web dashboard for followers, from any browser.

iPhone and Android work together seamlessly: chats, calls and alerts work across both platforms.

What Central does not do

  • It does not call emergency services: if you are in danger, always call 112 (or your local emergency number).
  • It does not offer video calls.
  • It does not track your location continuously and shows no ads.
