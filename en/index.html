Personal safety

Knowing you're OK, without keeping tabs on you

Features designed for people who live alone, get home late or work on the move. Nobody follows you without your say-so, and you can disconnect whenever you like.

Free

From the app, the widget or the quick settings tile, even with your screen locked. Your emergency contacts instantly get your location and a 30-second video.

  • Prompt to call 112 (the European emergency number)
  • Resent automatically if there's no signal
  • Recipients can take charge of the alert
Free

"I'm OK" check-in

Pick a time with the people who care about you. One tap says "I'm OK"; if your confirmation doesn't arrive, an alert goes out. It works even if your phone is switched off.

  • Reminder before the deadline

FAQ

Answers to the most common questions

Is Central free?

Yes. Encrypted chat, SOS, check-ins and Safe Trip are free. The Plus and Premium plans add more emergency contacts, more people looking out for you, safety perimeters, longer history and a live map in the web dashboard.

Does Central work on iPhone?

For now, Central is available for Android on Google Play. The web dashboard for the people who look out for you works on any computer.

Are my messages really private?

Yes. Messages, photos and trip locations are end-to-end encrypted: the keys stay on your phones and not even Central can read them. There are no ads and your data is never sold.

Who can see where I am?

Only the people you choose, and only when you send an SOS, start a trip or leave an agreed safety perimeter. Central doesn't track you continuously, and you can disconnect from anyone at any time.

What happens when I press SOS?

Your emergency contacts instantly receive your location and a short video, along with a prompt to call 112. Central does not replace emergency numbers.

Where is my data stored?

On servers in Europe. You can export your data or delete your account at any time from the app or the web dashboard.
