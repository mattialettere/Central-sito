Who it's for

You have the clients. We bring the technology.

Security

Firms with a control room: SOS and check-ins for guards on duty alone, patrols and site guarding, a live map for the control room.

Telecare

Services for older people living alone and their families: daily check-in, SOS from the widget, alerts to relatives and to your control room.

Cooperatives and businesses

Home care, cleaning, maintenance, insurers and consultants: lone worker protection with exportable history.

What you get

All of Central, under your brand

One-tap SOS

From the app, the widget or the lock screen: location and a short video sent to the chosen people.

Scheduled check-ins

Agreed times, weekly ones too. If the confirmation doesn't arrive, an alert goes out, even if the phone is switched off.

Safe Trip

Live location until arrival, and an alarm if the expected time is exceeded. Copes with patchy coverage.

Safety perimeters

Agreed areas, like a building site or a client's home: an alert on leaving and on returning.

Encrypted chat and calls

End-to-end encrypted messages, groups, photos and calls, instead of work WhatsApp groups.

Managing your clients

Each client with its own plan, its own guardians and staff, automatic invitations and renewal reminders.

Web dashboard

Live map, alerts with acknowledgement, check-ins, safety perimeters and history exportable to CSV, on your domain and in your colours.
