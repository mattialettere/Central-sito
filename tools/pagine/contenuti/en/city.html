In short: Central City is the town inside the Central app. Every user has a home to furnish, walks the streets with a joystick and bumps into their friends, who only show up moving around town if they want to. Coins for the shop come from small jobs in town and from using Central. It's free, and you can try it in your browser without signing up.

Why a town in a chat and safety app

Central is about staying in touch with the people you care about. The chat and safety tools matter most when something is needed; Central City is a reason to drop by on quiet days too: a place to build together, with the same friends as in your chats and no strangers.

What you can do in Central City

  • Your home: furnish it with items from the shop. You can also drop in and visit your friends' homes.
  • The streets: move your character with the joystick, as day, sunset and night change the light.
  • Your friends: each of them has a home in town. You only see them moving around if they've chosen to be visible.
  • Small jobs: restaurant, market, petrol station and fruit stall. Each job has a daily coin limit, so nobody has to play for hours to keep up.
  • Vegetable and flower gardens: plant, wait and harvest.
  • Pets: the home shop also has pets, such as a dog.

Coins, invites and subscriptions

You earn coins from small jobs and by inviting friends: whoever invites gets 100 coins for each confirmed friend, and the new user gets 200 coins and 3 days of Plus.

Central City is free. The Plus and Premium subscriptions, which mainly give you more contacts and zones in the safety features, also add something in town: coins every month (800 with Plus, 1,000 with Premium), shorter waiting times and a few extra items.

Try it now

Central City also opens in your browser, without signing up. To get your own home and find your friends you need the Central app, free for iPhone and Android.

What about privacy?

Only your friends on Central appear in town, that is people whose friend request you accepted, and you only see them moving if they want you to. People you've blocked don't appear. Your phone's real location has nothing to do with it: in Central City you move with a joystick, not GPS.
